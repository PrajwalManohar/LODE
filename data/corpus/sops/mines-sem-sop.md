# Mines SEM Lab Standard Operating Procedure

## Sample Preparation — Carbon Coating

Station 2B: Carbon coater. Uncoated specimens must be coated before SEM session. 5mm × 5mm steel samples: 90 second coating at 10 mA. Verify coating thickness with test slide if image charging occurs. Allow 15 min chamber pump-down after coating.

## Instrument Warm-up

SEM warm-up procedure: 30 min. Steps: (1) Turn on filament, (2) Pump chamber to <5×10⁻⁴ Pa, (3) Set accelerating voltage to operating value, (4) Auto gun alignment, (5) Calibrate EDS detector. Do not open chamber during pump cycle.

## Safety and PPE

Safety glasses required in SEM bay. No loose clothing near chamber. Metallic samples with reactive surface treatments require EH&S consultation before imaging.

## Shutdown Checklist

Return stage to home position. Vent chamber only after filament off and temperature stabilized. Log session hours in VEIN post-run report.
